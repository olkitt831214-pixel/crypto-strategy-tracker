Strategy Rules

Entry Conditions (all must be true at the daily close)

  • 15-day SMA above 40-day SMA — The faster moving average is above the slower one, indicating short-term upward momentum.
  • 3-day confirmation — The 15>40 condition (and price above 200 SMA) has been true for 3 consecutive daily closes. Buy on the 3rd day.
  • Price above 200-day SMA — The coin is in a longer-term uptrend.
  • 100-day SMA rising (15-day lookback) — The 100-day SMA is higher today than it was 15 days ago. Confirms the longer-term trend is still rising.
  • Bitcoin filter — Bitcoin itself is above its 200-day SMA and its 15-day SMA is above its 40-day SMA. The broad market must be in an uptrend.

Exit Conditions (any one is enough)

  • 15-day SMA falls below 40-day SMA — Short-term momentum has reversed.
  • Price closes below 200-day SMA — The longer-term uptrend has ended.
  • 30% hard stop — Price closes 30% or more below the entry price.
  • Variable ATR trailing stop (aggressive) — A trailing stop that tightens as profits increase:
    ProfitATR Multiple
    Below +25%2.5× ATR
    +25% to +50%1.5× ATR
    Above +50%1.0× ATR
    The stop only moves up, never down. This locks in more of the winners as the trade progresses.

Additional Guidance

  • Priority ranking — When multiple coins signal, prefer the ones with the highest 30-day Rate of Change (ROC). Lower priority numbers are stronger.
  • Volume filter — Prefer coins with 30-day average daily volume above $10M (shown in green). Lower volume increases slippage risk.
  • TOP badge — Marks the original 13 large-cap coins used in early backtests.
  • Long only — Never short. Cash is the defence when conditions are not met.
  • No leverage — Equal-weight the qualifying coins (or the top 4–6 by ranking).
  • Check once per day — After the daily candle closes (00:00 UTC). Act only on closed candles.
